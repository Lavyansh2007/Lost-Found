About the Lost & Found Portal

The Lost & Found Portal is designed to help people report lost belongings, browse reported items, and reconnect owners with their valuables through a simple and secure platform.

Our Mission

To provide an easy, reliable, and organized platform where lost items can quickly find their way back to their rightful owners.

Built With

Built using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB to demonstrate a complete full-stack web application.

Key Features

  • Report Lost Items
  • Browse Lost Items
  • Reply to Reports
  • Mark Items as Found
  • Admin Management
